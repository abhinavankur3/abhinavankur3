### 🏔️ About me

A software developer from the **Indian Himalayas**, building a life around **code, homelabs, and horsepower**.

I share what I actually use — home server builds, self-hosting guides, dev setups, 3D prints, and the occasional ride through **Uttarakhand** on my **ZX-10R**.

> Practical, no-fluff content for developers who want to build cool things and live well while doing it.

- 🌍 Based in India
- 🧪 Tinkering with homelabs, self-hosting & 3D printing
- 🛵 Weekend rides through the Himalayas
- 💌 Reach me at [abhinavankur.9@gmail.com](mailto:abhinavankur.9@gmail.com) or [abhinavankur.com](https://abhinavankur.com)
